# Dmart-Sales-Dashboard
An interactive DMart Sales Dashboard built using Microsoft Excel to analyze retail sales performance across categories, sub-categories, regions, segments, and shipping modes. The dashboard leverages pivot tables, charts, and slicers to enable dynamic filtering and deliver actionable business insights for decision-making.



# 📊 DMart Sales Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an **interactive DMart Sales Dashboard** created after analyzing retail sales data. The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of sales performance across multiple dimensions such as **Category, Sub-Category, Region, Segment, Ship Mode, State, and Time Period**. The objective is to derive actionable business insights and demonstrate data analysis & visualization skills.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze sales trends across different business dimensions
* Identify top-performing categories, regions, and customer segments
* Enable interactive filtering for deeper analysis
* Present insights in a clear and business-friendly dashboard format

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies Used

* **Microsoft Excel** (Data Cleaning, Pivot Tables, Charts, Slicers)
* **Data Analysis Techniques** (Aggregation, Filtering, Trend Analysis)
* **Dashboard Design Principles** (Visual hierarchy, consistency, readability)

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features

* **Category-wise Sales** – Visual breakdown of sales by product category
* **Sub-Category-wise Sales** – Bar chart showing detailed product performance
* **Region-wise Sales** – Comparison of sales across regions
* **Segment-wise Sales** – Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office analysis
* **Ship Mode-wise Sales** – Sales distribution by shipping methods
* **Interactive Slicers**:

  * Ship Date (Year & Month)
  * State-wise filtering
  * Sub-Category filtering

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

* Certain sub-categories contribute significantly higher sales compared to others
* Regional performance varies notably, indicating location-based demand patterns
* Consumer segment contributes the largest share of overall sales
* Standard shipping mode dominates total sales volume
